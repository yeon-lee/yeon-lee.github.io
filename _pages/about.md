We study the intersection of quantum many-body physics and quantum information science: what entanglement, information, and error correction tell us about many-body quantum matter, and what many-body physics tells us about large, noisy, or dynamically evolving quantum systems. Current directions include [mixed-state phases of matter](/research/#information), [quantum error-correcting codes](/research/#error-correction), [non-equilibrium dynamics and quantum control](/research/#dynamics), [topological and correlated materials](/research/#topology), and [exotic quantum phases and criticality](/research/#criticality).

The group is led by [Jong Yeon Lee](/bio/) and is part of the Anthony Leggett Institute of Condensed Matter Theory and IQUIST. See the [group](/group/) page for current members; prospective students and postdocs interested in these topics are welcome to get in touch.
