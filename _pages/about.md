In our group at the University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign, we explore the intersection of quantum many-body physics and quantum information science. Currently, our group is working on several interesting projects in

- Exotic quantum phases (topological phases, strange metals, etc.)
- Many-body physics of information and decoherence-induced physics
- Quantum error correction codes
- Non-equilibrium dynamics

See the [research](/research/) page for an overview of each direction, the [publications](/publications/) page for the full paper list (kept in sync with arXiv automatically), and the [group](/group/) page for current members. Prospective students and postdocs interested in these topics are welcome to get in touch.

**Brief bio.** I received a B.S./M.S. in Physics and Mathematics from Caltech (2011–2015, advisor: [John Preskill](http://theory.caltech.edu/~preskill/)) and a Ph.D. in Physics from Harvard University (2015–2020, advisor: [Ashvin Vishwanath](https://www.physics.harvard.edu/people/facpages/vishwanath)). I was a Gordon and Betty Moore Postdoctoral Fellow at the Kavli Institute for Theoretical Physics (2020–2023) and a Research Scholar at UC Berkeley (2023–2024) before joining the faculty at Illinois in 2024, where I am a member of the Anthony Leggett Institute of Condensed Matter Theory and IQUIST. See the [CV & talks](/cv/) page for more.
