New preprint: [Design Principles for Ultra-High-Rate Quantum Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30069) — Design rules for compact quantum LDPC codes, with column weight as the key knob, giving codes such as [[90,21,11]] and [[200,43,20]] that need only a few physical qubits per logical qubit.
