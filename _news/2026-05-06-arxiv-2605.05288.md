New preprint: [Charge Scrambling in Strong-to-Weak Spontaneous Symmetry Breaking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05288) — Shows when strong-to-weak symmetry breaking forces extensive charge fluctuations, and introduces a twist-overlap correlator that separates coherent from incoherent charge fluctuations.
