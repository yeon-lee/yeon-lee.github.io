New preprint with Bowen Shi: [Approximate Quantum Error Correction at Chiral Topological Edges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06258) — The chiral edges of topological phases act as approximate quantum error-correcting codes, with coherent-information loss set by universal edge properties and power-law-range recovery maps.
