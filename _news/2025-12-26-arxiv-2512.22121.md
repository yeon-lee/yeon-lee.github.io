New preprint with Akash Vijay: [Information Critical Phases under Decoherence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22121) — Decohered Z_N toric codes with N > 4 host an 'information critical phase' with a diverging Markov length, where a finite fraction of the logical information survives between the decodable and non-decodable phases.
