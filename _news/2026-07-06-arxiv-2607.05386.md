New preprint: [Logical Spectroscopy: Lifted-Product Codes with Addressable Bases](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05386) — Splits the group algebra of Abelian lifted-product codes into finite-field 'packets' to produce a complete, addressable basis of logical operators for codes with thousands of qubits.
