Jong Yeon is one of five Software Track winners of [Microsoft's Quantum Pioneers Program](https://quantum.microsoft.com/en-us/insights/blogs/announcing-latest-winners-and-finalists-of-the-quantum-pioneers-program), for the project *From Logical Spectroscopy to Hardware-Aware qLDPC Compilation*, which builds on the group's work on [logical spectroscopy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05386). The award comes with a $200,000 gift from Microsoft.
