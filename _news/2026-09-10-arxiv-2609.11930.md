New preprint with Akash Vijay: [Hierarchy of Rényi Coherent Information in Stabilizer Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11930) — Proves that the Rényi coherent information of stabilizer codes under Pauli noise never decreases with the Rényi index, and gives it an operational meaning through syndrome postselection.
