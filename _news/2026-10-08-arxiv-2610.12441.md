New preprint with Akash Vijay: [Short-Range Vector Spin Glasses: Parisi Hierarchies and Real-Space Ultrametricity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12441) — A local lattice model of vector spins that, at large N, hosts stable one-step and full Parisi replica-symmetry-breaking phases, with the Parisi hierarchy showing up directly as correlation lengths in real space.
