New preprint with Tai-Hsuan Yang, Andrew D. Kim and Akash Vijay: [Stability of Quantum LDPC States under Local Pauli Noise](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22357) — Proves that the code-space state of any qLDPC code stays stable under local Pauli noise: its conditional mutual information decays exponentially across a buffer, at both weak and strong noise.
