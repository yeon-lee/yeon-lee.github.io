New preprint: [Monte Carlo Studies of Twisted Bilayer Graphene: Strain and Thermal Fluctuations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20619) — Sign-problem-free quantum Monte Carlo maps the phase diagram of twisted bilayer graphene versus twist angle, strain and temperature, from a Dirac semimetal to the Kramers intervalley-coherent phase.
