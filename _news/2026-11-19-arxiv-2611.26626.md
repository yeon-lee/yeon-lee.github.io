New preprint with Andrew D. Kim and Tai Hsuan Yang: [High-temperature Gibbs states are uniformly Markovian and trivial](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26626) — We prove that, above a constant temperature threshold, Gibbs states of local noncommuting qudit Hamiltonians exhibit exponential decay of mutual information and conditional mutual information (CMI).
